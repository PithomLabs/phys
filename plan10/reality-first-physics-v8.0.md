# 0. Executive Thesis

The previous BM–Tate–AS program began with a suspected relationship:

$$
\text{BM}
+
\text{Tate/adelic mathematics}
+
\text{Asymptotic Safety}.
$$

That architecture was useful. It forced us to make mathematical objects explicit, type proposed bridges, construct null models, separate compatibility from interaction, and keep the physical coupling question visible.

But it also contained a structural bias.

Once Tate was admitted as a core architectural element, the program naturally tended to ask:

> Where can Tate fit?

That is not the right starting question.

The new starting point is:

$$
\boxed{
\textbf{What does physical reality actually require?}
}
$$

Only after the physical requirements are mapped should mathematics be selected.

Therefore v8.0 begins from four components:

$$
\boxed{
\begin{array}{lll}
\textbf{Dancer} &:& \text{matter / energy, accessed through observables}\\
\textbf{Dance}  &:& \text{dynamics / mechanism}\\
\textbf{Stage}  &:& \text{spacetime / geometry / causality}\\
\textbf{Mathematics} &:& \text{language used to describe, calculate, constrain, and test them}
\end{array}}
$$

The first three are the physical decomposition.

Mathematics is not introduced as a fourth physical substrate. It is the collection of languages through which the physical components are expressed and investigated.

Invariants are not a fifth physical component.

They are **cross-component fingerprints**: quantities, structures, or relations that survive an admissible change of description and therefore help connect the dancer, dance, and stage.

The v8.0 research question is therefore:

$$
\boxed{
\textbf{What is physically there, what makes it change, what spacetime structure
does it require, and what mathematics is actually forced upon us by those facts?}
}
$$

Only then:

$$
\boxed{
\textbf{What remains unexplained, and does any additional mathematics
remove that residual without increasing the assumption burden?}
}
$$

That is the program.

# 1. How We Got Here

The history of the program matters because v8.0 is not a cosmetic rename. It is the residue of several rounds of adversarial testing.

## 1.1 The original impulse

The early program asked whether arithmetic might lie beneath physics.

A discrete or arithmetic structure seemed attractive because mathematics repeatedly reveals surprising relationships among:

- integers;
- spectra;
- topology;
- symmetry;
- local-global principles;
- modular forms;
- L-functions;
- quantum systems.

From that observation came attempts to construct an arithmetic substrate and connect it to quantum mechanics.

The initial architecture was correspondingly ambitious:

$$
\text{microscopic arithmetic structure}
\rightarrow
\text{quantum amplitudes}
\rightarrow
\text{continuum}
\rightarrow
\text{physical observables}.
$$

The program then became progressively more careful as each arrow was tested.

---

## 1.2 The first major lesson: generation is not representation

A mathematical structure can reproduce a feature without generating the physical mechanism responsible for the feature.

This distinction appeared repeatedly.

An operator can have the right spectrum.

A topology can reproduce a quantized integer.

A symmetry can classify states.

A number-theoretic identity can reorganize amplitudes.

None of those facts alone proves that the corresponding mathematics is the mechanism by which nature produces the phenomenon.

The crucial distinction became:

$$
\boxed{
\text{representation}
\neq
\text{constraint}
\neq
\text{mechanism}.
}
$$

This distinction remains a foundational v8.0 principle.

---

## 1.3 The second major lesson: different mathematics has different native jobs

The Reality-First reference assembled separate maps of where different mathematical languages shine and where they become silent.

The resulting picture is not that mathematics is one giant interchangeable toolbox.

Different mathematical structures are naturally good at different physical questions.

Algebra is powerful when the physical content lies in:

- relations;
- composition;
- representations;
- symmetry;
- noncommutativity;
- consistency constraints.

Calculus is powerful when the physical content lies in:

- change;
- variation;
- flow;
- fields;
- continuous dynamics.

Geometry is powerful when the physical content lies in:

- distance;
- curvature;
- local structure;
- causality;
- spacetime.

Topology is powerful when the physical content lies in:

- global connectivity;
- deformation-invariant classes;
- defects;
- protected phases.

Spectral theory is powerful when physical information is encoded in:

- eigenvalues;
- resonances;
- operator spectra;
- response poles.

Statistics and probability are powerful when the physical question concerns:

- distributions;
- ensembles;
- fluctuations;
- thermodynamic behavior.

Arithmetic becomes especially interesting when the physical invariant itself possesses genuine arithmetic rigidity.

This gave us the methodological principle:

$$
\boxed{
\textbf{The physical question selects the mathematics.}
}
$$

not:

$$
\boxed{
\textbf{The preferred mathematics selects the physical question.}
}
$$

---

## 1.4 The third major lesson: arithmetic repeatedly appeared at interfaces

The arithmetic investigation did not end with “arithmetic is useless.”

Instead it revealed a more specific pattern.

Arithmetic appeared most naturally around highly structured quantities such as:

- spectra;
- partition functions;
- modular behavior;
- exact indices;
- L-functions;
- global arithmetic invariants;
- reciprocity relations;
- selected protected quantities.

At the same time, repeated attempts to make arithmetic directly generate generic continuous dynamics did not establish a successful route.

This led to the working hypothesis:

$$
\boxed{
\text{Arithmetic may be unusually natural for certain invariants,
without being the generic engine of dynamics.}
}
$$

This remains a hypothesis to be tested case by case.

It is not a universal theorem about all physics.

# 2. The Four-Component Physical Map

v8.0 adopts the following working decomposition.

## 2.1 The Dancer — Matter / Energy

The dancer is the physical content of the system.

This includes, depending on the system:

- particles;
- fields;
- excitations;
- collective degrees of freedom;
- matter;
- radiation;
- energy-carrying states.

But the dancer is not identical to an observable.

An observable is how the dancer becomes experimentally accessible.

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{
\text{Dancer}
=
\text{physical degrees of freedom}
\quad\text{accessed through observables}.
}
$$

The program must distinguish:

$$
\text{physical variable}
\neq
\text{observable}
\neq
\text{representation of the observable}.
$$

This prevents a mathematical coordinate from being silently promoted to a physical degree of freedom.

### Core questions

1. What is actually measured?
2. What physical degrees of freedom are required to explain the measurements?
3. Which variables are redundant?
4. Which quantities are direct observations and which are inferred?
5. Which properties are universal and which belong only to a particular system or state?

---

## 2.2 The Dance — Dynamics / Mechanism

The dance is change.

It is the answer to:

$$
\boxed{
\text{What makes the system evolve from one physical state to another?}
}
$$

Depending on the domain, the mechanism may be:

- a Hamiltonian;
- a Lagrangian or other variational principle;
- differential equations;
- field equations;
- an effective action;
- a scattering operator;
- a stochastic generator;
- a kinetic equation;
- a constrained evolution law;
- a causal update rule;
- a renormalization-group flow;
- a collective instability.

The program does not assume that every system has one universal type of dynamics.

The hard requirement is simpler:

> **If a theory claims to explain change, it must identify the mechanism responsible for the change.**

A spectrum is not automatically a mechanism.

A symmetry is not automatically a mechanism.

A conserved quantity is not automatically a mechanism.

A classification is not automatically a mechanism.

The mechanism must be independently identified.

---

## 2.3 The Stage — Spacetime

The stage is the physical arena in which matter and dynamics are realized.

At minimum this concerns:

- dimensionality;
- metric structure;
- causal structure;
- locality;
- topology;
- boundary structure;
- gravitational backreaction;
- whether geometry is fixed or dynamical.

The theatre metaphor must not be taken literally.

In general relativity, the stage participates in the play.

So the correct relation is:

$$
\boxed{
\text{Dancer}
\leftrightarrow
\text{Dance}
\leftrightarrow
\text{Stage}.
}
$$

Matter-energy contributes to spacetime dynamics, while spacetime constrains what matter can do.

The v8.0 question is not simply:

> What is the deepest geometry?

It is:

> **What geometric and causal structure is actually required by the observed physics, and which parts of that structure are assumed, derived, effective, or still unknown?**

---

## 2.4 Mathematics — The Language of Physics

Mathematics occupies a different role.

It is not assumed to be an additional physical ingredient.

It is the language through which we:

- define physical structures;
- calculate their behavior;
- prove relations;
- classify possibilities;
- express symmetries;
- identify invariants;
- compare representations;
- derive predictions.

Thus:

$$
\boxed{
\text{Mathematics}
=
\text{language}
+
\text{representation}
+
\text{calculation}
+
\text{proof}.
}
$$

A mathematical structure earns physical status only when the physics gives it a physical job.

This is why the v8.0 program is not “anti-arithmetic,” “anti-algebra,” “anti-geometry,” or “anti-NCG.”

It is **job-first**.

# 3. Invariants Are the Interface Layer

Invariants remain central, but their role is revised.

They are not a fourth or fifth component of physical reality.

They are the cross-cutting structures that help us compare the three physical components.

For example:

$$
\text{Dancer}\leftrightarrow\text{Dance}
$$

may be connected through spectral data.

$$
\text{Dancer}\leftrightarrow\text{Stage}
$$

may be connected through statistics, state-space structure, spin, topology, or geometry, depending on the system.

$$
\text{Dance}\leftrightarrow\text{Stage}
$$

may be connected through symmetry, conservation laws, causal structure, field equations, or geometric constraints.

The program therefore studies:

$$
\boxed{
\text{physical component}
\rightarrow
\text{interface structure}
\rightarrow
\text{invariant}
}
$$

rather than assuming:

$$
\text{invariant}
\rightarrow
\text{fundamental ontology}.
$$

---

## 3.1 What makes an invariant interesting?

Not simply that it is discrete.

Not simply that it is an integer.

Not simply that it is difficult to calculate.

Not simply that it can be written using number theory.

The important question is:

> **What physical transformations leave it unchanged, and why does that invariance matter?**

This distinction is vital.

Discrete does not automatically mean arithmetic.

Integer-valued does not automatically mean topological.

Algebraic does not automatically mean causal.

Global does not automatically mean topological.

An invariant becomes scientifically interesting when its preservation has an identifiable physical origin.

# 4. The Central v8.0 Objective

The previous question:

$$
\text{Can Tate be integrated with BM?}
$$

is no longer the master question.

The new objective is:

$$
\boxed{
\textbf{
Determine the minimum physical and mathematical structure required
to explain observed matter/energy, its dynamics, and its spacetime setting.
}
}
$$

Then:

$$
\boxed{
\textbf{
Identify the residuals that remain unexplained by that minimum structure.
}
}
$$

Then:

$$
\boxed{
\textbf{
Test whether additional mathematics removes that residual with less,
equal, or greater independent assumption cost.
}
}
$$

This creates a disciplined expansion rule:

$$
\text{known physics}
\rightarrow
\text{unexplained residual}
\rightarrow
\text{candidate structure}
\rightarrow
\text{new constraint}
\rightarrow
\text{prediction}.
$$

A candidate that does not begin from a residual remains interesting mathematics, not an admitted physical extension.

# 5. The v8.0 Research Spine

The program is organized as a sequence of physical questions.

## W0 — Reality Inventory

Start with the phenomenon.

Record:

- what is measured;
- measurement uncertainty;
- relevant physical regime;
- existing successful description;
- known symmetries;
- known conservation laws;
- known scales;
- established anomalies or residuals;
- unresolved pieces.

Output:

$$
\boxed{\text{Reality Ledger}}
$$

No speculative new mathematical layer is required at this stage.

---

## W1 — Dancer Census

Determine the physical degrees of freedom.

For each system:

$$
\text{observables}
\rightarrow
\text{states}
\rightarrow
\text{degrees of freedom}
\rightarrow
\text{redundancies}.
$$

This work distinguishes:

- what exists physically;
- what is merely a coordinate;
- what is a gauge redundancy;
- what is an effective degree of freedom;
- what is an inferred state variable.

The goal is to avoid inventing hidden variables before establishing that the existing physical description leaves something unresolved.

---

## W2 — Dance Reconstruction

Reconstruct the mechanism.

Ask:

$$
\boxed{
\text{What causes the observed change?}
}
$$

Determine:

- equations of motion;
- interaction terms;
- variational structure where applicable;
- response functions;
- conservation laws;
- dissipative terms;
- stability;
- scale dependence.

The output is a **mechanism map**.

The map must make clear which ingredients are:

- measured;
- inferred;
- derived;
- assumed;
- fitted.

---

## W3 — Stage Reconstruction

Determine the spacetime requirements.

Ask:

- What dimensionality is required?
- Is the metric fixed or dynamical?
- What causal structure is required?
- Which features depend on the metric?
- Which are metric-independent?
- What topology is physically relevant?
- Are boundary conditions essential?
- Does matter alter the stage?

The output is a **stage map**.

This prevents a mathematical space from being declared fundamental merely because it is convenient.

---

## W4 — Native Mathematics Map

For each physical task, identify its natural mathematical language.

A provisional example:

| Physical question | Candidate native mathematics |
|---|---|
| Continuous change | Calculus / analysis |
| Symmetry and generators | Algebra / representation theory |
| Local shape and curvature | Geometry |
| Global deformation-invariant structure | Topology |
| Operator spectra and resonances | Spectral theory |
| Distributions and fluctuations | Probability / statistics |
| Information and computation | Information theory / complexity |
| Exact numerical/global rigidity | Arithmetic / number theory |

This table is not an ontology.

It is a routing system.

The same physical system may legitimately require several mathematical languages.

---

## W5 — Interface and Invariant Atlas

Study the three physical interfaces:

$$
D\leftrightarrow Dn,
\qquad
D\leftrightarrow S,
\qquad
Dn\leftrightarrow S
$$

where, schematically,

- \(D\) = dancer,
- \(Dn\) = dance,
- \(S\) = stage.

For each interface, identify:

- the physical relation;
- the mathematical representation;
- the invariant;
- the reason it survives;
- the observables affected;
- what changes under perturbation;
- what does not.

The objective is not to force all interfaces into one pattern.

It is to determine whether there is a repeated structural law.

---

## W6 — Residual Hunt

After the existing physical theory and its native mathematics are fully mapped, ask:

$$
\boxed{
\text{What is still unexplained?}
}
$$

A residual must be specific.

Examples include:

- a missing parameter relation;
- an unexplained degeneracy;
- a protected but unexplained quantity;
- a persistent cross-regime relation;
- a mismatch between descriptions;
- an unaccounted causal influence;
- a UV inconsistency;
- a failure of continuum recovery;
- a missing selection rule.

The residual must not be manufactured by imposing an alternative mathematical language first.

---

# 6. Mathematics Admission: When a New Language Enters

A candidate mathematics enters v8.0 through a funnel.

## Level 0 — Applicability

Does the physical problem possess the mathematical structure that the candidate requires?

If not:

$$
\boxed{\text{candidate rejected for this case}.}
$$

---

## Level 1 — Utility

Does the candidate express the residual more naturally than the incumbent mathematics?

This establishes usefulness.

It does not establish physical necessity.

---

## Level 2 — Structural Gain

Does the candidate reveal a constraint, invariant, or equivalence that was not visible in the incumbent description?

Still not enough.

It may simply be a better representation.

---

## Level 3 — Explanatory Reduction

Does the candidate reduce independent assumptions or free parameters?

Now the candidate begins to compete with the null.

---

## Level 4 — Predictive Leverage

Does the candidate derive a relation or prediction not supplied as input?

This is the first serious threshold.

---

## Level 5 — Null Separation

Does the result survive the strongest non-candidate explanation with equal or lower assumption cost?

Only then does the new mathematics earn serious physical promotion.

The program therefore uses:

$$
\boxed{
\text{Applicability}
\rightarrow
\text{Utility}
\rightarrow
\text{Constraint}
\rightarrow
\text{Rigidity}
\rightarrow
\text{Prediction}
\rightarrow
\text{Null separation}.
}
$$

---

# 7. Occam's Razor as Explanatory Compression

Occam's razor is not interpreted as “fewest equations.”

The relevant quantity is **independent explanatory structure**.

A theory becomes more attractive when it explains more with less independent freedom.

We therefore keep an explicit assumption budget.

A proposed extension can introduce:

- new degrees of freedom;
- new constants;
- new arbitrary functions;
- new boundary conditions;
- new coupling maps;
- new representation choices;
- new sector identifications;
- new regularization prescriptions;
- new selection principles.

Every one of these costs explanatory freedom.

The candidate must therefore answer:

$$
\boxed{
\text{What do we gain for what we add?}
}
$$

The strongest improvement is:

$$
\boxed{
\text{fewer independent assumptions}
+
\text{greater explanatory coverage}
+
\text{new prediction}.
}
$$

That is the v8.0 meaning of simplicity.

---

# 8. How v8.0 Intends to Beat Null Models

The program does not try to beat weak nulls.

It builds the null from the same physical problem.

For a candidate \(C\), compare at least conceptually against:

\[
N_0=\text{established physical explanation},
\]

\[
N_1=\text{different representation of the same explanation},
\]

\[
N_2=\text{native non-candidate mathematics},
\]

\[
N_3=\text{generic extension with comparable degrees of freedom},
\]

\[
N_4=\text{equal-flexibility alternative with comparable parameter freedom},
\]

\[
N_5=\text{best realistic competing mechanism},
\]

\[
N_6=\text{candidate ablation}.
\]

The exact null set is system-dependent.

The principle is universal:

$$
\boxed{
\text{The null must receive enough freedom to make failure meaningful.}
}
$$

---

## 8.1 What counts as beating a null?

Not merely:

\[
\chi^2_C<\chi^2_N.
\]

A more complicated theory will often fit better.

The meaningful question is:

$$
\boxed{
\text{Does the candidate explain a held-out feature
with no unjustified increase in independent freedom?}
}
$$

Possible wins include:

- an exact relation replacing several fitted parameters;
- a prediction made before the data are inspected;
- a cross-regime constraint;
- a structural explanation shared by independent systems;
- a protected quantity the null cannot derive;
- an observable difference caused by changing the candidate structure while holding the ordinary physics fixed.

A candidate that merely fits existing data more flexibly has not beaten the null.

# 9. The Arithmetic Policy in v8.0

This section is deliberately explicit.

## Tate is not a foundational module.

Tate mathematics is no longer part of the default physical architecture.

Neither is:

- adelic completion;
- motives;
- motivic Galois theory;
- the cosmic Galois group;
- noncommutative geometry;
- asymptotic safety.

All remain available as candidate mathematical frameworks.

But they must enter through physical necessity.

The program now asks:

$$
\boxed{
\textbf{Does the physical residual have a structure for which
Tate mathematics is genuinely native?}
}
$$

If yes, Tate enters.

If no, it does not.

---

## 9.1 When Tate may become relevant

Tate-like mathematics becomes a legitimate candidate when a physical system independently exhibits structures such as:

- a genuine local-global organization;
- a canonical arithmetic parameter;
- a nontrivial arithmetic spectrum;
- a global invariant with arithmetic rigidity;
- a canonical Green/height construction;
- a mathematically forced adelic decomposition;
- an established correspondence whose physical endpoint is independently known.

The key word is:

$$
\boxed{\textbf{independently}.}
$$

We do not construct a physical residual merely to give Tate somewhere to live.

---

## 9.2 What does not qualify

These are insufficient by themselves:

- an integer appearing in a calculation;
- a discrete spectrum;
- a p-adic model existing in mathematics;
- an adelic identity;
- a modular form appearing in a partition function;
- a zeta function appearing in a trace;
- an attractive analogy to a physical potential;
- the possibility of writing a tensor product;
- a beautiful parent-object diagram.

These may motivate investigation.

They do not establish physical necessity.

# 10. The Role of BM After the Revision

Bohmian mechanics remains important, but its role is more carefully scoped.

BM is a **successful physical benchmark and a concrete quantum ontological representation in the nonrelativistic regime**.

It therefore supplies a powerful reference point for questions involving:

- actual configurations;
- guidance dynamics;
- wavefunction evolution;
- quantum equilibrium;
- nonrelativistic trajectories;
- interference;
- measurement statistics.

But v8.0 no longer requires the entire research program to terminate in BM.

Instead:

$$
\boxed{
\text{BM is one empirically anchored physical realization against which
candidate extensions can be tested where BM is the appropriate regime.}
}
$$

This preserves one of the strongest lessons from the earlier program:

> Do not replace a successful physical description merely because a new mathematical language is available.

# 11. The Role of Asymptotic Safety After the Revision

Asymptotic Safety is likewise demoted from architecture to candidate scale framework.

Its natural question is:

$$
\boxed{
\text{What happens to the effective theory as the scale changes?}
}
$$

That makes it relevant when the residual concerns:

- UV consistency;
- fixed points;
- critical surfaces;
- universality;
- scaling;
- relevant and irrelevant directions.

But:

$$
\boxed{
\text{AS is not automatically the UV ontology.}
}
$$

Nor does the existence of a motivic or arithmetic structure automatically imply that it is an AS coupling.

If an arithmetic direction appears in an RG description, v8.0 asks:

- Is it physical or redundant?
- Is it relevant or irrelevant?
- Does it affect an observable?
- Does a non-arithmetic direction reproduce the same effect?
- Is its role universal or scheme-dependent?

# 12. Parent Structures: Open Hypothesis, Not Foundation

The recent convergence of NCG, endomotives, motives, adelic geometry, periods and renormalization remains intellectually important.

But v8.0 changes the wording.

We do not begin with:

$$
\mathfrak P=\text{the parent of physics}.
$$

We ask:

$$
\boxed{
\text{Do independent physical systems force us toward a common parent structure?}
}
$$

A candidate parent structure becomes more compelling only if multiple independent physical problems converge on the same mathematical organization.

The evidence would look like:

$$
P_1\rightarrow\mathfrak P,
\qquad
P_2\rightarrow\mathfrak P,
\qquad
P_3\rightarrow\mathfrak P
$$

where the physical problems were selected without assuming \(\mathfrak P\) in advance.

Even then, the parent structure remains a mathematical hypothesis until it acquires physical leverage.

# 13. The Research Program's Central Question

The old program asked:

> Can arithmetic underlie physics?

The revised program asks:

> **Where, if anywhere, does a mathematical structure become physically indispensable?**

The strongest form is:

$$
\boxed{
\textbf{
Can a mathematically canonical structure remove genuine physical freedom,
produce a held-out prediction, and survive an equal-flexibility non-candidate explanation?
}
}
$$

This question applies to arithmetic.

It also applies to:

- algebra;
- geometry;
- topology;
- information theory;
- category theory;
- quantum computation;
- noncommutative geometry;
- any future mathematics.

That is why v8.0 is not an arithmetic program with a new name.

It is a **general program for discovering what mathematics physics actually requires**.

# 14. Case-Study Strategy

The program should now prefer a small number of carefully selected physical systems over one giant universal construction.

Each case should be chosen because it presents a clean relationship among dancer, dance, stage, and mathematics.

A calibration set may include:

### Quantum mechanics

For example:

- harmonic oscillator;
- hydrogen;
- interference;
- simple scattering.

Purpose:

Determine precisely where operator algebra, calculus, spectral theory, probability and BM each perform indispensable jobs.

### Topological quantum matter

For example:

- quantum Hall systems;
- topological insulators;
- anyonic systems.

Purpose:

Determine when topology and algebra genuinely become physically active and when arithmetic is only a description of the resulting invariants.

### Integrable systems

Purpose:

Study the rare cases where algebra comes unusually close to determining dynamics.

### Quantum field theory

Purpose:

Separate symmetry, renormalization, dynamics, anomalies, representation structure and observables.

### Gravitational systems

Purpose:

Determine what geometry actually contributes to the mechanism rather than simply to the language.

### Strongly structured spectral systems

Purpose:

Identify physical situations in which arithmetic-rich spectra are not engineered by construction but arise from an independently motivated system.

These are not claims that one of these systems will solve the program.

They are laboratories for determining the dividing lines among mathematical languages.

# 15. The Long-Term Research Question: Structural Compression

The deepest objective of v8.0 is not “find the most fundamental object.”

It is:

$$
\boxed{
\textbf{compress the explanation without erasing the physics}.
}
$$

Suppose several apparently unrelated laws turn out to follow from a small number of independent structural principles.

Then:

$$
\text{many laws}
\longleftarrow
\text{few principles}.
$$

That is genuine explanatory compression.

But the reverse danger must also be watched.

If one “unifying” framework requires:

- many special definitions;
- many auxiliary sectors;
- several arbitrary identifications;
- numerous tuned parameters;
- multiple exceptional cases;

then the apparent unification may simply be a larger description of the same data.

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{
\text{unification}
\neq
\text{addition}.
}
$$

A genuine unification should reduce independent explanatory commitments.

# 16. What “Theory of Everything” Means in v8.0

v8.0 does not begin by assuming that a TOE is one equation.

Several meanings remain possible.

A TOE might mean:

1. one dynamical law;
2. one underlying mathematical structure;
3. one unified interaction;
4. one scale-flow;
5. a complete structural map.

The program does not decide these possibilities in advance.

Its narrower commitment is:

> A successful final theory must compress independent physical facts without secretly importing the facts as assumptions.

Thus an eventual TOE-level result could take the form:

$$
\boxed{
\text{small set of principles}
\rightarrow
\text{matter}
+
\text{dynamics}
+
\text{spacetime}
+
\text{observables}
}
$$

Or it could instead show that the three-component structure cannot be reduced further.

Both outcomes would be scientifically meaningful.

# 26. The Final v8.0 Charter

The research program can now be stated compactly.

### Principle 1 — Reality first

Start from observed physical behavior.

### Principle 2 — Physical decomposition

Separate:

$$
\text{matter/energy},
\qquad
\text{dynamics},
\qquad
\text{spacetime}.
$$

### Principle 3 — Mathematics by job

Use the mathematical language demanded by the physical question.

### Principle 4 — Invariants as interfaces

Treat invariants as cross-component fingerprints rather than automatically as ontology.

### Principle 5 — Residual before extension

Do not introduce new mathematics until an explicit unexplained residual exists.

### Principle 6 — Occam by explanatory freedom

Count independent assumptions, not equations.

### Principle 7 — Equal-flexibility nulls

A candidate must face a serious non-candidate alternative with comparable explanatory freedom.

### Principle 8 — Prediction before promotion

A new structure must produce a consequence not used to construct it.

### Principle 9 — No privileged mathematics

Tate, NCG, motives, AS, BM and every future candidate remain subordinate to the physical question.

### Principle 10 — Failure is knowledge

A killed bridge remains a useful boundary result.
